About me

My academic background and hands-on project experience have equipped me with expertise in programming languages like Python, SQL, and R, as well as a deep understanding of data structures, algorithms, and the mathematical principles behind machine learning and deep learning.

I ensure that data is not only accessible but also presented in a way that drives informed decision-making.
